# Privacy Policy — MinasGeraisPublisher

Last updated: November 20, 2026

MinasGeraisPublisher respects your privacy. This application is used solely by its owner (`@ohminasgeraismg`) to publish videos to their own TikTok account.

1. **Data Collected:** Only the OAuth access token and basic profile identifier (`open_id`) provided by TikTok Login Kit are stored locally to authorize video uploads.
2. **Data Sharing:** No user data is sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.
3. **Data Deletion:** Users may revoke access at any time from their TikTok account settings or by deleting local credentials.
4. **Contact:** silas.silva@ifmg.edu.br
